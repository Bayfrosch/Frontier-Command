# Frontier Command - General Building Roles

## Overview

General Building Roles define the core infrastructure available to all factions.

While individual factions may use different names, appearances, and mechanics, every faction should fulfill these essential building functions.

Buildings form the foundation of a player's economy, military production, technological development, and territorial control.

---

# Design Philosophy

Buildings should have clear and meaningful purposes.

Every structure should answer one of the following questions:

* How do I gather resources?
* How do I expand?
* How do I produce units?
* How do I unlock technology?
* How do I defend territory?

Buildings should create strategic choices rather than unnecessary complexity.

---

# Command Structure

## Purpose

Acts as the primary command center of a faction.

## Responsibilities

* Starting structure
* Produces Construction Units
* Provides basic faction functionality
* Possible victory condition

## Characteristics

* Most important building
* Expensive to replace
* Critical strategic target

Every player begins the match with one Command Structure.

---

# Resource Processing Structure

## Purpose

Processes gathered Materials.

## Responsibilities

* Produces resource gather units
* Accept harvested resources
* Generate material income
* Support economic growth

## Characteristics

* Economic backbone
* Usually located near resource fields

Expansion often requires additional processing structures.

---

# Energy Production Structure

## Purpose

Generates Energy.

## Responsibilities

* Power advanced infrastructure
* Support research

## Characteristics

* Essential for technological development
* Strategic target during warfare

Players must continuously expand energy production as their infrastructure grows.

---

# Production Structure

## Purpose

Produces military units.

## Responsibilities

* Unit construction
* Army development
* Military reinforcement

## Characteristics

* Core military infrastructure
* Multiple versions may exist

Examples:

* Infantry facilities
* Vehicle factories
* Aircraft facilities

---

# Research Structure

## Purpose

Unlocks new technologies.

## Responsibilities

* Research projects
* Doctrine progression
* Technology advancement

## Characteristics

* Expensive investment
* Critical strategic choice

Research structures represent long-term power growth.

---

# Defensive Structure

## Purpose

Protect territory and infrastructure.

## Responsibilities

* Defend bases
* Delay enemy attacks
* Secure strategic locations

## Characteristics

* Effective force multiplier
* Cannot replace armies

Defenses should support military forces rather than become the primary method of warfare.

---

# Detection Structure

## Purpose

Provides battlefield awareness.

## Responsibilities

* Reveal enemy movement
* Detect hidden units
* Support strategic planning

## Characteristics

* Information-focused
* Important for map control

Knowledge of the battlefield is a strategic advantage.

---

# Capital Infrastructure

## Purpose

Supports Capital Units and advanced military assets.

## Responsibilities

* Capital unit production
* Capital upgrades
* Strategic military development

## Characteristics

* High-tech structure
* Significant investment

Usually appears during the mid or late game.

---

# Superstructure

## Purpose

Represents the pinnacle of faction technology.

## Responsibilities

* Unlock endgame capabilities
* Enable advanced projects
* Support powerful strategic options

## Characteristics

* Very expensive
* Late-game investment

Not every match will require construction of a Superstructure.

---

# Expansion Philosophy

Buildings should naturally encourage territorial growth.

Players should expand because:

* Resources are limited.
* Outposts provide strategic benefits.

Expansion should be rewarded but also create new vulnerabilities.

---

# Building Progression

A typical match should follow a progression similar to:

1. Command Structure
2. Resource Infrastructure
3. Energy Infrastructure
4. Initial Production
5. Expansion
6. Research Infrastructure
7. Advanced Production
8. Capital Infrastructure
9. Endgame Infrastructure

This progression creates a natural transition from economic development to large-scale warfare.

---

# Desired Player Experience

Players should feel that every building contributes to the growth of their military frontier.

Bases should evolve from a small foothold into a network of economic centers, production facilities, research complexes, and military strongholds capable of supporting large-scale warfare.
