# Frontier Command - Vision Document

## Overview

Frontier Command is a real-time strategy game that combines base building and tactical battlefield combat of classic RTS games with the long-term progression and fleet customization found in modern grand-scale strategy games.

The goal is to create a game where players establish forward bases, secure resources, develop advanced technology, command powerful armies, and deploy customizable special units that evolve throughout a match.

## Vision

Frontier Command aims to deliver large-scale warfare without sacrificing tactical control or battlefield readability.

Combat should be immediate, responsive, decisive but most important good to understand. Building an army, expanding territory, and engaging enemy forces should remain the core focus of gameplay.

## Inspirations

### Command & Conquer

Inspired by:

* Fast-paced combat
* Responsive unit control
* Base building
* Resource gathering
* Clear battlefield readability
* Distinct faction identities

### Sins of a Solar Empire

Inspired by:

* Research systems
* Technology progression
* Capital ship progression
* Mid and late-game strategic development

## Design Goals

### Battlefield

The battlefield is the center of the experience.

Players should spend most of their time commanding armies, expanding territory, and fighting opponents rather than navigating between disconnected locations.

The Battlefield should be a consistent map with obstacles and depth

### Meaningful Progression

Technology and veteran units should create a sense of progression throughout a match.

Players should develop unique strategies through research choices and flagship development.

### Customizable Flagships

Powerful capital-class units should gain experience, level up, and unlock specialized abilities during a match.

These units should become valuable strategic assets rather than disposable combat units.

They should be high risk, high reward because a loss should be expensive but also mean to lose time through expierience and upgrades

### Strategic Expansion

Territory control and resource acquisition should drive conflict and encourage aggressive expansion.

Expansion should create opportunities for combat rather than isolate players from one another.

It should be rewarding to expand instead of defend a single point
