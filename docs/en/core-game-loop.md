# Frontier Command - Core Gameplay Loop

## Overview

A match of Frontier Command is divided into three phases:

1. Early Game - Establish the Frontier
2. Mid Game - Contest the Frontier
3. Late Game - Break the Frontier

Each phase should naturally transition into the next while maintaining constant pressure on players to expand, adapt, and engage in combat.

The game should reward proactive decision-making and discourage passive defensive play.

---

# Early Game - Establish the Frontier

## Objective

Create a functioning base and secure the resources needed for future expansion.

At the start of a match, players focus on constructing critical infrastructure and establishing their initial economy.

During this phase, players make their first strategic decision:

* Expand rapidly to claim territory and resources.
* Invest in infrastructure and technology to strengthen their initial position.

Neither option should be strictly superior.

Players who expand aggressively gain access to more resources but become vulnerable to attack.

Players who focus on development gain stronger infrastructure but risk losing map control.

Spare resources should force a Player to choose one option instead of going for both immediately

## Gameplay Focus

* Base construction
* Economy setup
* Initial scouting
* First technology choices
* Expansion planning

## Desired Player Experience

"I have established my base and now need to decide how I will grow stronger than my opponent."

---

# Mid Game - Contest the Frontier

## Objective

Secure territory, establish borders, and determine strategic advantage.

The mid game begins when opposing factions begin to interact directly.

Players establish forward positions, defend critical resource locations, and test each other's strengths through raids, skirmishes, and limited offensives.

This phase should provide players with information about:

* Enemy strategy
* Military strength
* Expansion patterns
* Technology choices

Territory becomes increasingly important.

Players must maintain enough military power to defend occupied zones while continuing economic and technological development.

## Gameplay Focus

* Territorial control
* Forward base construction
* Army composition
* Research progression
* Border conflicts
* Resource managment

## Desired Player Experience

"I understand my enemy's strengths and weaknesses and must decide how to gain the advantage before the decisive battles begin while manageing my resources."

---

# Late Game - Break the Frontier

## Objective

Achieve victory through decisive military action.

The late game should not become a static defensive stalemate.

By this stage, players have developed their economy, technology, and military forces.

The focus shifts from expansion toward decisive strategic operations.

Large-scale battles should determine the outcome of the match.

A player who made better strategic decisions throughout the game should gain meaningful advantages, but victory should still require effective battlefield execution.

## Gameplay Focus

* Major offensives
* Capital unit deployment
* Siege operations
* Strategic breakthroughs
* Large-scale battles

## Desired Player Experience

"This is the moment everything has been building toward. The choices I made throughout the match are now being tested in a decisive conflict."

---

# Preventing Late-Game Stalemates

A major design goal of Frontier Command is to prevent situations where heavily fortified players can no longer effectively attack one another.

The game should encourage decisive action rather than passive defense.

Possible systems include:

* Limited strategic resources that eventually become contested.
* Increasing rewards for map control.
* Powerful late-game breakthrough units.
* Long-range siege options.
* Economic incentives for expansion.
* Research paths designed to counter static defenses.

Defensive structures should delay an attack, not permanently stop one.

No defensive position should be completely unbreakable.

---

# Match Narrative

Every match should tell a clear story:

1. Build a frontier.
2. Expand the frontier.
3. Defend the frontier.
4. Contest the frontier.
5. Break the enemy frontier.
6. Achieve victory.
