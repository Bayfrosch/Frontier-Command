# Frontier Command - Factions & Research Philosophy

## Overview

Frontier Command features three primary factions.

Each faction represents a distinct military philosophy and battlefield approach.

While factions have unique units, structures, technologies, and visual identities, all factions follow the same fundamental gameplay principles:

* Territorial expansion
* Base construction
* Resource gathering
* Research progression
* Large-scale warfare

The goal is to create factions that feel distinct while remaining understandable and balanced.

---

# Faction Design Philosophy

Every faction should:

* Have clear strengths and weaknesses.
* Be recognizable at a glance.
* Support multiple playstyles.
* Remain competitive throughout all game phases.

Faction differences should primarily come from:

* Unique units
* Unique structures
* Unique defenses
* Unique technologies
* Unique doctrine choices

Faction identity should not rely primarily on numerical bonuses.

---

# The Three Factions

## Frontier Coalition

The adaptable human faction.

### Core Identity

The Coalition represents military flexibility, logistics, and battlefield adaptability.

They excel at responding to changing situations and maintaining balanced armies.

### Characteristics

* Flexible unit roster
* Strong logistics
* Balanced economy
* Adaptable strategies
* Reliable technology

### Weaknesses

* No extreme specialization
* Few overwhelming advantages
* Requires strategic flexibility

### Battlefield Role

The Coalition rewards players who adapt to their opponent and maintain balanced military forces.

---

## Iron Dominion

The industrial and heavily militarized faction.

### Core Identity

The Dominion represents overwhelming firepower, industrial production, and defensive strength.

Their armies are slower but exceptionally powerful.

### Characteristics

* Heavy armor
* Strong defenses
* Powerful production capability
* Durable military forces
* Strong territorial control

### Weaknesses

* Lower mobility
* Slower expansion
* Higher operational costs

### Battlefield Role

The Dominion rewards players who establish strong positions and gradually overpower their opponents.

---

## Ascendancy

The advanced technology faction.

### Core Identity

The Ascendancy relies on superior technology, energy systems, and elite military assets.

Their armies are smaller but highly specialized.

### Characteristics

* Advanced technology
* Powerful abilities
* Elite units
* Superior research
* Strong capital units

### Weaknesses

* Expensive infrastructure
* Smaller armies
* Greater dependence on research

### Battlefield Role

The Ascendancy rewards players who invest in technology and leverage superior battlefield capabilities.

---

# Research System Philosophy

Research is one of the most important strategic systems in Frontier Command.

Research should create new possibilities rather than simply improve existing numbers.

Research choices should influence how a faction is played and what tools become available.

Players should frequently face meaningful decisions between:

* Building armies
* Expanding territory
* Advancing technology

A player cannot efficiently maximize all three simultaneously.

---

# Research Progression

Research is divided into two phases.

---

## Phase One - Core Research

All players begin with access to the same faction-specific core technology tree.

This phase focuses on:

* Basic economic upgrades
* Fundamental military improvements
* Core infrastructure
* Essential technologies

The purpose of this phase is to establish the faction's foundation.

---

## Phase Two - Doctrine Selection

After reaching a defined research milestone, players must choose one of two doctrines.

This decision represents a strategic commitment.

Each doctrine unlocks exclusive technologies, structures, units, and abilities.

Some options become unavailable after choosing a doctrine.

The choice should significantly influence the remainder of the match.

* Heavy armor
* Vehicle warfare

* Frontline combat

* Siege vehicles

* Flexible battlefield response

---

* Energy weapons

---

# Doctrine Design Rules

Every doctrine should provide:

* New units
* New structures
* New defenses
* New technologies
* New strategic options

Doctrine choices should never be limited to passive percentage bonuses.

---

# Desired Player Experience

Players should feel that selecting a doctrine changes the identity of their faction for the remainder of the match.

The same faction should be capable of pursuing different strategies depending on the doctrine chosen, creating multiple viable playstyles while maintaining a clear faction identity.
