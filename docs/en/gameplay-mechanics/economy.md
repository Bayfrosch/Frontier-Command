# Frontier Command - Economy, Resources, Research & Base Building

## Overview

Frontier Command combines the accessible economy of classic real-time strategy games with meaningful strategic progression and territorial expansion.

The goal is to create an economy that is easy to understand while allowing players to specialize regions of their territory according to their strategic needs.

Players must constantly balance military production, economic expansion, and technological advancement.

---

# Resources

Frontier Command uses two primary resources.

## Materials

Materials represent raw industrial resources used for physical construction and manufacturing.

Used for:

* Buildings
* Vehicles
* Infantry
* Defensive structures
* Capital units

Materials are gathered from resource fields located throughout the map.

Harvesters collect resources and transport them to refineries.

This system is directly inspired by the simplicity and readability of Command & Conquer.

---

## Energy

Energy represents power generation, advanced technology, and logistical support.

Used for:

* Research
* Special abilities
* High-tech structures
* Discovery and intel

Energy is generated through power infrastructure.

Advanced economies require significant energy production.

A technologically advanced faction will often consume more energy than a militarily focused faction.

---

# Territorial Economy

The battlefield is divided naturally through expansion and territory control.

Players can conquer Outposts to secure new regions.

Outposts are secured requireing a small army so expansion is limited by resources and time needed for the conquest

Outposts function as regional command centers.

---

## Outpost Specialization

Each Outpost may be specialized.

Players can choose a specialization after capturing which reduces and improves factors to give economy decisions more depth.

see more in map-territory.md

---

This system creates strategic economic choices without requiring players to manage complex economy systems.

# Research

Research is a major strategic investment.

Research should be a passive background activity but with high impact.

Every research choice should compete directly with military production and expansion.

A player investing heavily in technology accepts temporary military weakness in exchange for long-term advantages.

---

## Research Principles

Research should:

* Unlock new strategic options
* Introduce new units
* Introduce new structures
* Improve existing systems
* Create meaningful specialization

Research should not primarily consist of small percentage increases.

---

## Research Paths

Each faction possesses multiple research branches.

A player cannot efficiently complete every branch during a match.

Choosing a research path represents a strategic commitment.

Every faction has two main research Paths effectifely splitting it into two seperate factions after researching a shared core research tree

Example:

### Military Doctrine

Focuses on:

* Combat units
* Weapon systems
* Armor improvements
* Battlefield abilities

---

### Advanced Technology

Focuses on:

* Energy systems
* Capital unit upgrades
* Experimental technology
* Economic improvements

---

# Economic Decision Making

One of the central decisions of Frontier Command is resource allocation.

Players continuously decide whether resources should be spent on:

* Army production
* Economic expansion
* Research
* Defensive infrastructure

The strongest players will be those who correctly identify when to shift resources between these competing priorities.

This tension should remain present throughout the entire match.
